About Prasanna Pet Clinic

Dedicated To Preserving The Joy of Healthy Pets

Founded with a mission to bring compassionate, cutting-edge veterinary healthcare under one roof. Our clinic is equipped with advanced ICU suites, digital X-rays, ultrasound, and dedicated sterile surgical operating theaters.

In-House Pathology

Complete blood count, biochemistry, and PCR diagnostics within 30 minutes for quick treatment decisions.

Overnight ICU Care

24-hour oxygen therapy, fluid monitoring, and continuous vital signs supervision for critical post-op pets.

Advanced Surgery

Orthopedic bone plating, spay/neuter, soft tissue procedures with inhalation anesthesia monitoring.

Exotic Pet Care

Specialized avian, rabbit, rodent, and reptile handling by certified exotic animal practitioners.
